# Introduction to Generative AI – Key Notes

## Artificial Intelligence (AI) Overview

AI simulates human intelligence using machines.

AI models learn from large datasets through a process called training.

## Two Fundamental Approaches

### 1. Discriminative AI

Learns to distinguish between different classes of data.

Uses labeled training data to predict the class of new data points.

Works by finding which side of a decision boundary a data point falls on.

Best suited for classification tasks (e.g., spam vs. non-spam emails).

Cannot understand context or generate new content.

### 2. Generative AI

Learns to generate new content based on training data.

Captures the underlying distribution of data to create novel instances.

Starts with a prompt (text, image, video, etc.) and outputs new content:

- \- Text, images, audio, video, code, data.

Can produce output in the same form (text-to-text) or different form (text-to-image).

Mimics creative skills, unlike discriminative AI which mimics analytical skills.

## Examples

Discriminative AI: ‘Is this image a nest or an egg?’

Generative AI: ‘Draw an image of a nest with three eggs.’

## Generative AI Models

Built using deep learning and artificial neural networks.

Key model types:

- \- GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)

- \- VAEs (Variational Autoencoders)

- \- Transformers

- \- Diffusion Models

These models are the building blocks of generative AI.

## Evolution of Generative AI

- 1950s: Early machine learning concepts.

- 1990s: Neural networks advanced generative AI.

- 2010s: Deep learning + large datasets + computing power accelerated progress.

- 2014: GANs introduced by Ian Goodfellow.

- 2018 onwards: Transformer-based models like GPT revolutionized text generation.

## Foundation Models

Broad capability models adapted for specialized use cases.

Large Language Models (LLMs):

- \- Understand and generate human language.

- \- Examples: GPT series (GPT-3, GPT-4), Google PaLM, Meta LLaMA.

Other generative models:

- \- Images: Stable Diffusion, DALL-E.

- \- Text tools: ChatGPT, Gemini.

- \- Images: MidJourney.

- \- Video: Synthesia.

- \- Code: Copilot, AlphaCode.

## Impact and Applications

Wide scope across domains and industries.

Enhances creativity and productivity.

McKinsey predicts trillions of dollars added to global economy through productivity gains.

## Key Takeaways

Generative AI creates new content based on learned data.

Creative capabilities come from models like GANs, VAEs, transformers, diffusion models.

Foundation models enable specialized tools for diverse applications.

Generative AI is transforming work and creativity globally.
